About Me

I'm a self taught web developer from Nairobi, Kenya. I got into tech because I have always loved creation and I wanted to create and bring things to life -- bringing the ideas in my head into reality.

I don't have much of a background before Tech. I started learning to code after finishing High School. I am finishing up on my degree in Computer Science in Multimedia University. I love it when tech-solutions are used to simplify every day tasks.

When I am not coding, you will find me working on some animations. I love art and happen to be a 3d artist as well. I love researching about the new developments in Technology and listening to podcasts from founders who have already made an impact in the world through technology.

I am currently looking for opportunities in web development --- whether that is a junior developer role, a freelance project, or a collaboration on something meaningful.
